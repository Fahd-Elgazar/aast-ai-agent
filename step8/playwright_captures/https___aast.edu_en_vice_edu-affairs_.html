Education & Students Affairs

Welcome to Education &
Students Affairs

The Education Sector constitutes the higher coordination authority between the various colleges and education and research sectors inside the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport.
At AASTMT Education Affairs, we are committed to providing you with the resources and assistance you need to thrive academically and personally. From course registration to academic counseling, we are here to guide you every step of the way. Our mission is to foster an environment that promotes curiosity, critical thinking, and personal growth.
