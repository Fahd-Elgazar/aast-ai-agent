AASTMT Alumni

Welcome to AASTMT
Alumni Center

The AASTMT Alumni Center was established in August 1998 and it was the 1st official entity within the university to provide a focused and real service to the graduates. The association was established with an objective to create a solid community and strengthen the bond between AASTMT and its alumni through creating a lifetime relationship. We aim to ensure that our graduates stay in contact with us and their fellow alumni to learn and share experiences.
