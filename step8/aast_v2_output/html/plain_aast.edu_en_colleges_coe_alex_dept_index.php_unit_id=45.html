What You'll Study

Electrical & Control Engineering is an important field of engineering which involves the study of operation, design and control of power systems, electrical drives and automatic control systems. The curriculum offers a wide range of subjects which enables graduates to work in a variety of fields. It provides opportunities to work on cutting edge technologies, contribute to sustainable energy solutions, and tackle real world challenges. It opens doors to diverse industries and offers a rewarding career path with high demand, innovation and the potential to make a significant impact on society.

Read more

Degrees Offered

The Electrical & Control Engineering department equips students with the knowledge and skills to design, analyze and manage electrical systems and control industrial processes. It covers topics such as power systems, instrumentation, renewable energy, electric vehicles, electrical machines, power electronics and control. These fields prepare graduates for careers in different industries such as energy, manufacturing, automation and robotics. The department also offers post graduate programs such Master of science, Master of engineering and philosophy of doctor degrees.

Market & Career
Opportunities

  • Power & Machines

    Power Engineering involves the generation, transmission & distribution of electrical energy, ensuring a reliable power source for various applications. Machines engineering encompasses the design, construction, operation and control of electrical machines that enable efficient operation across industries.

  • Smart Automation

    This rapidly evolving field combines cutting edge technologies with engineering principles to create intelligent and efficient systems. It involves the integration of sensors, actuators, control systems and artificial intelligence to automate and optimize various processes. Smart automation engineering finds applications in industries, manufacturing, healthcare, petroleum, food and beverage, and automotive. 

  • Energy

    This field focuses on the study, development and optimization of energy systems. It encompasses various areas, including conventional and renewable power generation, energy conservation and management. Engineers work on designing and improving energy efficient systems, exploring sustainable energy sources and developing strategies for reducing carbon emissions. Thus they play an important role in projects such as renewable power plants and energy storage solutions.
