My Neighbourhood, From Global Policies to Urban Design Interventions

  • NOV 07, 2024
  • Smart Village
  • General

The Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) is proud to be part of the global movement to create sustainable, inclusive, and resilient cities. AASTMT is actively engaged in the implementation of UN-Habitat's innovative "My Neighbourhood" framework.

This groundbreaking framework offers a multi-scalar approach to urban design, considering the interconnectedness of different urban scales, from the neighbourhood level to the city region. By emphasizing people-centred design, "My Neighbourhood" prioritizes the involvement of diverse stakeholders in the planning process.

The framework's adaptability and flexibility allow it to be applied to various urban contexts, ensuring that solutions are tailored to local needs and aspirations. AASTMT is committed to utilizing the "My Neighbourhood" framework to drive sustainable urban development in the region.
