        Why Pharmacy Alamein?

        Internationally Accredited Program

        Learning Experience complemented with regular Practical Training 

        State of the art Smart Campus  

        Exceptional Learning Experience 

        Contemporary Laboratories 

        We will Help You , Every Step of the Way

        At CLC, you will acquire the knowledge, abilities, and practical experiences necessary for your career. We will help you every step of the way to take that next step towards a brighter future and carreer.

        Internationally Accredited Program

        we were among the first Universities in Egypt to apply and successfully receive a Provisional International-Accreditation (Category 2) of the Bachelor of Pharmacy Degree by the Accreditation Council for Pharmacy Education (ACPE)

        Practical Training

        In order to Bridge the Gap between Theory and Practice, we implemented rigorous training scheme throughout the college years to help the students get a sense of the real-world experience before graduation

        Premium Services

        we offer a wide selection of Premium student-related services including 24-hour clinic, 5-star Restaurant, Modern Furnished Think Tanks, Top-line Dormitory, outdoor playground, Indoor Game room. 

        Clinical Simulation 

        Traditional Teaching Methods have become obsolete, we’re investing in Cutting-edge Technology for a more competent Graduate. New Teaching Methodologies equal better future

        Student Activities

        we provide unwavering support in encouraging our students to excel in extra-curricular activities by all means, including the introduction of Entrepreneurship training and the consecutive RALLY competition, access to students union and their various clubs
