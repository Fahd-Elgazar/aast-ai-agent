GSB is beyond education, it is an environment of performance shaping global leaders for tomorrow to be qualified for the real world. 

MBA International Programs

The International MBA caters to business professionals who recognize that the most significant challenges are those we personally establish. The program revolves around an exceptional structure that prioritizes the strengths, requirements, and objectives of every individual involved.

MBA
Master of Business Administration

A Master of Business Administration (MBA) is an advanced academic qualification that equips individuals with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills relevant to business and investment management. The program aims to enhance graduates' comprehension of various aspects of general business management, enabling them to pursue prosperous and fulfilling careers in professional management while also serving as a source of inspiration for future global business leaders.


DBA
Doctorate of Business Administration

The Doctorate of Business Administration (DBA) is designed for students who wish to pursue a professional career. The DBA is focused on both theory and applied research and will help the students to acquire leadership skills as an executive, consultant, or entrepreneur.

BDC
Business Development Center

The Business Development Center (BDC) is an essential entity that plays a pivotal role in fostering the progress and advancement of businesses. The DBC offers invaluable aid by providing solutions to various challenges, enabling businesses to gain access to capital, foster the exchange of innovative technologies, and enhance their overall business.

DBA/PhD
International Programs 

The DBA/PhD focuses mainly on the application of theoretical knowledge to the advancement of management and business practices. It is internationally recognized as the most appropriate post-MBA program as it combines academic research and business practices to further the development of personal and professional skills.
