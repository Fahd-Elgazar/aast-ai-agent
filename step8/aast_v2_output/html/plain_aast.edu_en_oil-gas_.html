Oil and Gas Hub

Oil and gas hub is created by ASSTMT to support oil, gas and energy industries locally and regionally.

The hub provides a variety for B.Sc. Programs in oil and gas engineering, chemical and petrochemical engineering, petroleum and energy logistics. Moreover, MBA and DBA in oil and gas, Master in quality management are also provided. Professional training courses, OPITO approved, for safety (MEMIMR, OIM, BOSEIT…etc.) and work force development are also available. Specialized training using simulators for oil pollution as well as offshore vessels’ dynamic positions did exist.
