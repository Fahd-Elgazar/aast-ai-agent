We will Help You , Every Step of the Way

At CLC, you will acquire the knowledge, abilities, and practical experiences necessary for your career. We will help you every step of the way to take that next step towards a brighter future and carreer.

International Programs

Benefit from the culturally rich opportunities for studying abroad offered by CLC at a number of reputable international universities for both media and translation students.

Practical Training

CLC provides students with a variety of local and international training opportunities at highly reputable institutions. As part of the program requirements, each student must undertake a minimum of 60 hours of field training in specialized institutions, ensuring that they gain hands-on professional experience in their field of study

Services & Facilities

Enjoy a range of facilities including specialized labs, studios, plateau and classrooms with state-of-the-art equipment, in addition to several cafeterias, playgrounds, library and clinic.

Training Courses

CLC offers a number of specialized courses in the fields of Language, Media, cinema techniques and Translation.

Student Activities

College students are encouraged to participate in a variety of activities aimed at helping them develop their perceptions of who they are and what they are capable of, in preparation for their future professional roles in society.
