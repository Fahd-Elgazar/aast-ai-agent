College of International Transport and Logistics, Lataki

The College of International Transport and Logistics (CITL) in Latakia, Syria, was established at the AASTMT Latakia branch in 2008.
the College of International Transport and Logistics in Latakia serves as a key educational hub in Syria, providing specialized training and qualifications in the vital field of logistics and global trade, under the umbrella of the well-regarded Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport.

The program aims to produce competent graduates who can handle modern trends in the global economy, understand the globalization of production and investment, and work in various sectors such as internal and external trade, industry, banking, insurance, oil companies, and all types of transport companies (land, sea, air), ports, warehouses, container companies, and shipping agencies.

Trade Logistics and International Transport Management Program

The Trade Logistics and International Transport Management Program is designed to equip students with the essential skills and knowledge required to excel in the rapidly evolving field of logistics and international trade. This comprehensive program offers specialized majors that focus on different facets of logistics management, providing students with both theoretical insights and practical experience.
