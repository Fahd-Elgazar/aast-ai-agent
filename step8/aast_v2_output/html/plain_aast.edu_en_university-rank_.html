AASTMT University Ranking

AASTMT Ranking

The Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) has established itself as a leading institution in both regional and global university rankings, reflecting its commitment to excellence in education, impactful research, and alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

AASTMT is regularly recognized in major international systems such as the QS World University Rankings, the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings, and the THE Impact Rankings, where the Academy consistently achieves top positions nationally and strong placements regionally. It is also ranked in the QS Subject Rankings, particularly in engineering and tecnology fields, underscoring its academic strengths.

Within the Arab region, AASTMT is an active participant in the Arab Ranking for Universities (ARU), where it is acknowledged among the region's leading institutions for teaching quality, research activity, and community engagement.
These achievements reflect AASTMT's long-term strategy (2024-2027) to enhance research output, foster international collaboration, and build a reputation as a regional hub with growing global influence.
