About Smart Village Campus

Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport

In the middle of the contemporary hub of Business Technology at Smart Village is located the newest branch of the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport.
AASTMT established this state-of-the-art Campus in 2012. With the Three Colleges open for ambitious students from all over the Arab World, we hope to offer them the same top quality education we always did, added to it exceptional advantages of Summer Training Opportunities, Internship Programs and Job Offers from the hundreds of Multinational Companies within this elite business commuity.
