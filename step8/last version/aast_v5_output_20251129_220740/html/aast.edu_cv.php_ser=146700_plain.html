biography

Assistant Professor at Arab Academy for Science & Technology (AASTMT). I obtained my Ph.D. in the Electronics and Electrical Engineering Department, University of Strathclyde, Glasgow, United Kingdom, in December 2022. I obtained my B.Sc.; and M.Sc. degrees in Electronics and Communications Engineering from AASTMT, Alexandria. I am interested in Medical Image Processing, especially detecting and classifying breast cancer. I believe that my research will help to improve early detection of breast cancer.
