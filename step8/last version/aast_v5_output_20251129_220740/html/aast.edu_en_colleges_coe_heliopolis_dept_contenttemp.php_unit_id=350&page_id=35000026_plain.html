Laboratories

The program utilizes a number of well-equipped laboratories for instructions and research. Hands-on experience is highly emphasized throughout the program and ultra modern equipments serve the interests of both the students and staff. The laboratories serving the program are:

  1. Hydraulic Laboratory.
  2. Construction Surveying Laboratory.
  3. Soil Mechanics Laboratory.
  4. Construction Materials Laboratory.
  5. Structural Mechanics Laboratory.

Other laboratories are used commonly by all engineering students :

  1. Computer labs.
  2. Chemistry Laboratory.
  3. Electrical Labs.
  4. Physics Laboratory.
,

III. SOIL MECHANICS LAB

LABORATORY INFORMATION
  • Lab name: Soil Mechanics Lab
  • Room no.: 002A
  • Capacity: 15 students.
Major Equipment
  • Preparation and reefing equipment for samples for testing.
  • Liquid and Plastic limit device.
  • Cone pentameter, and Pocket pentameter.
  • Electronic top loading balance.
  • Two set of series 200 and 300 mm diameter.
  • Long stem soil hydrometer, Sedimentation cylinder, and Thermometers.
  • 250 C fan circulated oven.
  • Equipment for direct shear test: motorized direct shear apparatus, lever loading device, shear box assembly.
  • Equipment for one-dimensional consolidation: consolidation frame, dial gauge, consolidation cell.
  • Equipment for permeability of soil, constant head permeability cell, manometer tubes and stand, constant level tank, and measuring cylinder.
  • Equipment for dry density / moisture content: proctor mould , compaction rammer, straight edge, 200 mm sieve and receiver, semi-automatic balance.
  • Equipment for density of soil inplace, sand cone method : 6-in sand cone, plastic container, density plate, general purpose oven, field and laboratory scales, density spoon , sample container 10 dm3, rubber headed mallet, and density chisel.
  • Preparation and reefing equipment for samples for testing.
  • Liquid and Plastic limit device.
  • Cone pentameter, and Pocket pentameter.

V. STRUCTURAL DYNAMICS LAB

LABORATORY INFORMATION
  • Lab name: Structural Dynamics Lab
  • Room no.: 109A
  • Capacity: 15 students.
Major Equipment
  • Universal structural mounting frame 5000N capacity
  • Experimental set for investigating simple stability problems
  • Experimental set of beam on 2 supports for shear force & bending moment diagrams
  • Experimental set of parabolic arch
  • Experimental set for three hinged arch
  • Experimental set for testing deformation of frames
  • Experimental set for forces in suspension bridge
  • Experimental set of cable under dead-weight
  • Experimental set for forces in various single plane trusses
  • Experimental set for forces in Howe truss
  • Experimental set for forces in Warren truss
  • Experimental set for torsion of bars
  • Experimental set for free vibration of bars
