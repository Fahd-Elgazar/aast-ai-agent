The Ready For Tomorrow project, powered by AAST Entrepreneurship Centre and PLAN International Foundation

  • by: Entrepreneurship Center
  • Heliopolis Campus
  • General

With 1800+ participants, 1200+ trained, and 900+ new ideas & business models, we’ve paved the way for aspiring entrepreneurs in Cairo and Alexandria.Join us as we celebrate the success of the ready For Tomorrow project, powered by AAST Entrepreneurship Centre and PLAN International Foundation. 

This project opened the doors to many through:

  • Ideation to reality: Learning how to turn their ideas into action.
  • Business model mastery: Building the right framework for success.
  • Financial access: Gaining insights into securing funds for their busniness.
  • Exclusive networking: Connecting them with industry experts and like-minded individuals through networking events.
  • Seed funding secured: Culminate their journey by obtaining seed funding.
