biography

Hussein Mowafak is an accomplished Assistant Lecturer specializing in Accounting and Finance. Holding a Masters's degree in this field, he's currently pursuing his Ph.D. at The Arab Academy Graduated Studies in Business. His journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and Finance, fueling his passion for the subject. Joining the Arab Academy in 2008, Hussein's teaching prowess and expertise made him a beloved figure among students. His commitment to mentoring students and conducting impactful research has been notable throughout his career. His academic pursuits reflect his dedication to expanding knowledge in accounting and finance, His ongoing Ph.D. studies aim to contribute valuable insights to the field. Hussein leads an active lifestyle, relishing CrossFit workouts and gym sessions for fitness. He finds serenity in water sports like wind and kitesurfing. Additionally, Hussien values social interactions, embracing opportunities to connect and engage with diverse individuals, balancing his academic rigor with a vibrant, multifaceted life.
