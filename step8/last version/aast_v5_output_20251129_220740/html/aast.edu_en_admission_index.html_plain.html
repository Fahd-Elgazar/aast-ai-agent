Admission

We seek promising students who will contribute to the AASTMT community during their college years, and to society throughout their lives. Programs

The Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport offers its students the opportunity to earn curricula with a foreign degree in addition to that of AASTMT or a joint degree during the same study period.
