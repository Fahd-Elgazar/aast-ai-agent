What You'll Study

The Mechanical Engineering Department - Cairo Branch offers a variety of courses that help the students prepare to be easily hired in prestigious jobs that are offered to mechanical engineers in both the global and local market. 

For the MECHATRONICS concentration, from automation of existing mechanical systems to biomedical and robotic applications, we offer our students an upgrade to the conventional mechanical engineering experience to cope with the tremendous breakthrough seen lately in recent technologies...

For the MECHANICAL POWER concentration, from turbomachinery to HVAC systems and renewable energy applications , the program offers a full experience for students to merge between trivial energy conversion systems and new trends in order to catch up with the energy management plans adopted by nations worldwide...

Degrees Offered ...

The Mechanical Engineering Department offers the students a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering in either MECHATRONICS or MECHANICAL POWER concentration. In addition, there is a chance for students to keep developing their skills through graduate studies to achieve a masters degree or a doctoral degree if they wish to.

Market & Career
Opportunities

  • DESIGN, OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE

    Graduates can install, troubleshoot and repair specific equipment types by utilizing industrial technology. They can diagnose equipment failure and schedule preventive maintenance operations accordingly.

  • ROBOTICS , AUTOMATION AND CONTROL SYSTEMS

    Graduates can build, configure, test, and troubleshoot automated industrial and robotic systems. They can also develop automated systems control and advanced programming for data analysis than can be integrated with artificially intelligent systems.

  • PETROLEUM SECTOR

    Graduates can work on drilling, piping design, hydraulic systems design, and maintenance. They can also work with the exploration, extraction and production of oil.
