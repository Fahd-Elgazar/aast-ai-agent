Deanery of Student Affairs

The student affairs sector at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport essentially aims at developing an integrated administrative educational entity that seeks to serve all students through cultural, social and athletic activities. The sector also enriches students Á academic experience by playing a complementary role to the educational process at the Academy. This is achieved through providing high-standard student accommodation, catering, as well as a wide range of cafeterias in all the different Academy campuses.
