Horizons in Architecture and Urban Studies

Horizons in Architecture and Urban Studies (HAUS) Journal is dedicated to innovative research in the field of architecture and urbanism by providing a platform for high-quality, peer-reviewed research in architecture, urban studies, and interdisciplinary domains. The journal welcomes contributions that address Architectural and Urbanism research in the following areas:

  1. Design and Theory: Theoretical explorations and critical analyses of the built environment and design methodologies.
  2. Educational Approaches: Innovations in education theories and practices and pedagogical strategies.
  3. History and Conservation: Historical studies of architecture, preservation, and conservation of heritage.
  4. Sustainability: Research on sustainable design principles and practices, economic, social, and environmental sustainability, and green building technologies.
  5. Social and Cultural Aspects: Research in society and culture in relation to the built environment. Environmental Behavioral studies
  6. Technology: Advances in digital tools, construction, technologies, material science, and their applications.

HAUS encourages interdisciplinary research that bridges architecture and urbanism with other fields. welcomes research that reflects the unique challenges, opportunities, and cultural variations and nuances across the globe. Submissions should contribute to the advancement of knowledge, propose new frameworks, or offer critical insights into existing practices.

By fostering scholarly discourse, HAUS aims to inspire researchers, educators, and architects to push the boundaries of the discipline and address the challenges of the built environment in the 21st century.

This publication follows a quarterly schedule, with new issues published every three months. In addition to regular issues, HAUS aims to publish special issues dedicated to emergent and critical topics in the built environment. Guest editors will curate these special issues.

Inaugural Issue of HAUS

  

Inaugural Issue of HAUS

Horizons in Architecture and Urban Studies Journal

We are pleased to announce the launch of HAUS: Horizons in Architecture and Urban Studies Journal and invite scholars, researchers, and practitioners to submit their contributions for the First Issue. HAUS is a peer-reviewed journal dedicated to advancing research in architecture, urbanism, and interdisciplinary domains. Our mission is to provide a platform for innovative and high-quality scholarship that address critical issues in the built environment

Scope of the Journal

We welcome original research articles, theoretical explorations, and critical analyses in the following areas:

Design and Theory – Investigations into design methodologies, architectural theory, and critical discourse.

Educational Approaches – Innovations in pedagogy, architectural education, and new teaching strategies.

History and Conservation – Studies in architectural history, heritage preservation, and conservation practices.

Sustainability – Research on environmental, social, and economic sustainability in architecture and urban design.

Social and Cultural Aspects – Studies on the relationship between society, culture, and the built environment, including environmental behavior research.

Technology – Advancements in digital tools, construction technologies, material science, and their applications in architecture and urbanism.

HAUS encourages research that bridges architecture and urban studies with other disciplines, reflecting diverse global challenges, opportunities, and cultural perspectives. Contributions should offer new insights, theoretical frameworks, or critical evaluations of contemporary and historical practices.

Submission Guidelines

We invite researchers to submit original manuscripts for consideration in the first issue. Submissions should align with the journal’s focus areas and adhere to academic standards of originality and rigor through this link: https://apc.aast.edu/ojs/index.php/HAUS

Submission open July 2025.

Deadline for submissions 1st October 2025.

Expected publication date December 2025.

We look forward to receiving your contributions and engaging in meaningful discourse on the future of architecture and urban studies.
