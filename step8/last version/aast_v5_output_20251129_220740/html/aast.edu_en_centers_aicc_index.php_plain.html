Agreements & International Cooperation Center (AICC)
In our pursuit for excellence and continuous progress, we have envisioned the necessity to prepare our graduates to be internationally competent negotiators, policy-makers, politicians and entrepreneurs.

AASTMT administration has also recognized that our students must be exposed to different cultures speak different languages and experience different business practices in order to achieve success in an increasing by demanding academic and career market place.
