WHAT IS RALLY ACCELERATE PROGRAM ?


  • Rally Accelerate is an End-to-End fundraising program targeting Egyptian startups who are aiming to scale their business


  • Rally Accelerate is dedicated to fueling the success of startups in the investment phase, Our program offers international expert guidance, connections with key industry players, and access to a global network of investors to accelerate growth and success


  • Through the tailored mentoring, and strategic support, we help startups navigate the challenges of scaling their businesses and secure the funding needed to thrive in the competitive startup ecosystem
