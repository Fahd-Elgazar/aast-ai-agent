Electrical Energy Engineering

Objectives


 

The Electrical Energy program has three main program educational objectives (PEOs):

 

1- Secure, and succeed in, professional and responsible engineering positions within the industry

 

2- Be admitted to post-graduate studies at highly respected academic institutions.

 

3- Contribute to the discipline such as by participating in professional societies, publishing in journals, and attending conferences, workshops and professional seminars.
