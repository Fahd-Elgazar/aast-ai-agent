The Fourth edition of Language and Communication International Scientific Forum (LCR Forum 2025) at Smart Village Campus

  • NOV 26, 2025
  • AASTMT
  • General

The College of Language and Communication and the College of Engineering and Technology of the Arab Academy for Science and Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) at Smart Village Campus launched the Fourth edition of Language and Communication International Scientific Forum (LCR Forum 2025). The forum's theme, "Future Frontiers: Reimagining Human Expression through Emerging Technologies in Language, Media, and Art," presented an innovative perspective on the role of technology in shaping human creativity and created a framework to enhance discussion among specialists, scholars, and innovators.

The forum was held during the period from 24 – 26 November 2025, at Smart Village Campus, in the presence of H.E. Prof. Dr. Ismail Abdel Ghaffar Ismail Farag – AASTMT President, in addition to an elite of leading academics, experts, researchers, digital media pioneers, and technology innovators, with the participation of young people and a number of senior AASTMT Officials.

AASTMT organized the Digital Humanities and Cultural Heritage Exhibition 2025 alongside LCR Forum 2025 under the theme: “Innovations Reshaping Human Expression” as integrate human thought, artistic creativity, and digital technology. The exhibition highlighted the role of researchers and innovators in utilizing emerging technologies to serve culture, heritage, and knowledge. Additionally, the official forum competition has been launched. The competition will recognize the most outstanding creative, media, and research initiatives with excellence and innovation prizes in recognition of creativity, interactive media, and research in digital humanities and digital cultural transformation.
