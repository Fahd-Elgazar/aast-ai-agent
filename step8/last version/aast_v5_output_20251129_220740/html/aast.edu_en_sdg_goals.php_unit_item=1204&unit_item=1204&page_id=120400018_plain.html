    Goal 4. Quality Education

    4.3.2 Public events (lifelong learning) (Public lectures, Community Educational Events)

    2023-2024

    The Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) is dedicated to fostering lifelong learning and community engagement through a variety of complimentary events, including lectures, workshops, and conferences that tackle contemporary issues. The Academy enriches cultural life through art exhibitions and performances, collaborates on community priorities such as marine conservation, and provides accessible online education and support for individuals with disabilities.

    In the 2023-2024 academic year, AASTMT successfully organized over 40 international conferences and professional workshops, attracting more than 6,000 participants. The Academy places a strong emphasis on inclusivity and knowledge sharing to promote lifelong learning and sustainable growth. Collaborations with esteemed organizations like UNESCO and the World Bank enhance AASTMT's capacity to advance education and public engagement, aligning with Sustainable Development Goal 4.3.2. All events are free or open to the public and focus on themes of sustainability, gender equality, and youth empowerment.

    Public lectures, Community Educational Events

    2022-2023

    The Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) is dedicated to fostering lifelong learning and community engagement through a variety of free public events and initiatives. These offerings include public lectures by experts on diverse topics, community educational events such as workshops and training sessions aimed at skill development, and conferences and symposia that bring together professionals and the public to tackle key issues. Collaboration with local organizations ensures that events address community interests, such as marine conservation campaigns, while online learning platforms extend educational opportunities globally. Committed to accessibility and inclusivity, AASTMT provides accommodations for disabilities and multilingual resources, fostering a welcoming environment for all participants. Feedback from attendees drives continuous improvement, demonstrating AASTMT’s commitment to meaningful community connection and lifelong learning.
