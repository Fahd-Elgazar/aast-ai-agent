    Goal 1. No Poverty

    1.4.1 Local start-up assistance

    2023 -2024

    In alignment with Sustainable Development Goal 1 (No Poverty) and its mission to eradicate poverty in all its forms everywhere, the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) is dedicated to promoting economic empowerment, social inclusion, and equal access to opportunities and resources. Through its diverse training programs, entrepreneurship initiatives, and vocational education, AASTMT works to reduce vulnerability, address inequality, and strengthen social welfare and economic resilience. Since 2015, the AAST Entrepreneurship Center has advanced youth empowerment and innovation-driven start-ups across the Arab and African regions, generating income opportunities and sustainable livelihoods. The Institute of Technical and Vocational Studies complements these efforts by linking technical training with industry collaboration to enhance human capital and self-reliance. Moreover, the Community Service and Continuing Education Center extends AASTMT’s reach to marginalized and low-income communities through lifelong learning, skills-development, and social responsibility programs that promote employment, economic inclusion, and poverty alleviation. Collectively, these initiatives embody AASTMT’s commitment to inclusive development, social equity, and sustainable prosperity across the Arab region and beyond.

    AAST Entrepreneurship Center ​​​​​​​

    Mentorship programmes and training workshops provided by the AASTMT Entrepreneurship Center are always free. Access to the AASTMT facilities is always free.

    Start-up School

    AAST Startup School acts as the educational arm of the Entrepreneurship Centre. The main objective of this program is equipping early-stage entrepreneurs with the essential, substantial, and core knowledge & skills. On another side, supporting existing startups through advanced trainings and technical, industry-related mentorship programs. Also, the school provides AAST EC incubators with the required educational support to achieve the impactful incubation experience.

    Visit the Start-up school at the Entrepreneurship Centre at AASTMT

    Advanced Pre-Incubation Program

    Advanced Pre-Incubation Program is an an advanced pre-incubation training program for AAST students & alumni providing them with the necessary knowledge & support to put their ideas into action. The program starts with a validation exercise, then during this pre-incubation journey, students learn how to assess their business, compare between ideas to choose the best and develop a business model canvas. The program closes with a pitching exercise for a chance to join one of our incubators.

    AASTMT–ASRT Partnership to Promote Entrepreneurship and Innovation for Poverty Reduction

    In July 2024, the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Academy of Scientific Research and Technology (ASRT) to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and technological start-ups through business incubators, accelerators, and specialized training programs. This strategic partnership aims to empower young entrepreneurs and researchers with the resources, mentorship, and technical support needed to transform innovative ideas into viable economic enterprises.


    The initiative directly supports Sustainable Development Goal 1 (No Poverty) by promoting income generation, self-employment, and sustainable livelihoods, particularly among youth and recent graduates who face limited job opportunities. By facilitating access to entrepreneurial ecosystems and financial inclusion mechanisms, AASTMT and ASRT contribute to reducing the proportion of people living in poverty through productive employment and advocating equal access to economic resources and technology. This collaboration underscores AASTMT’s commitment to reducing poverty through inclusive economic growth and innovation-driven empowerment.

    AAST Entrepreneurship Center was established by The Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport, with the focus of empowering Arab youth to create a sustainable growing ecosystem in the Arab region & Africa. It aims to build generations of young entrepreneurs & change agents who reshape the social & economic status of the Arab region & Africa by enabling an environment of innovation, growth, support & mentorship for private businesses in AAST.

    Mentorship programs and training workshops provided by the AASTMT Entrepreneurship Center and Access to the AASTMT facilities are free for students.

    Start-up School

    AAST Startup School acts as the educational arm of the Entrepreneurship Centre. The main objective of this program is equipping early-stage entrepreneurs with the essential, substantial, and core knowledge & skills. On another side, supporting existing startups through advanced trainings and technical, industry-related mentorship programs. Also, the school provides AAST EC incubators with the required educational support to achieve the impactful incubation experience.


    Visit the Start-up school at the Entrepreneurship Centre at AASTMT

    Good Will Incubator for AAST Students [4]

    The 1st Business Incubator for Entrepreneurs with Disabilities, established in cooperation and sponsorship between Rowad 2030, project of the Ministry of Planning and Economic Development and the Entrepreneurship Center at the Arab Academy for science, technology and maritime transport. Goodwill Incubator is designed to support entrepreneurs with varying disabilities and startups that have innovative solutions for people with disabilities. Goodwill Incubator works on helping them develop their business ideas and turn it into real business through training, mentorship, consultation and prototype development.

    We believe that Entrepreneurship and self-employment is one of most viable fields for people with disabilities who are looking to enter the job market on their own terms.

     

    The first of its kind and also serves young people who offer solutions to people of determination. Supporting young people and selecting the best projects submitted by them, as well as networking between them and investors and stakeholders, and laying the foundations for an ecosystem specialized in supporting entrepreneurship and innovation for people with special needs.

    During the training workshop, an exhibition was held to support the participating entrepreneurs with disabilities to develop their entrepreneurial projects and startups. In addition, presenting an award for the best project at the exhibition and encouraging it to participate in the International "Access Abilities Expo in Dubai, 15-17, November 2022, in coordination with the Union of Arab chambers and the UNIDO office for Investment and Technology Promotion in Bahrain.

    Youth Incubator for AAST Students
    The Implementation Strategy Matrix of the Entrepreneurship Centre offers various services to help youth enhance and validate their prototypes. Through our trainings, consultancies, exposure, and one-to-one mentorship, they support youth to launch their dream startups, and compete in the International Market. With their sector’ focused incubation programs, they fill the gaps.
    The Youth Incubator at the Arab Academy is the incubation program designed for young entrepreneurs of students and fresh graduates, that aims to provide them with all needed support to start and grow innovation-based start-ups in different fields. The six months incubation program is tailored to meet the needs and challenges of early-stage entrepreneurs at that stage; it starts with a pre-incubation capacity building phase where students receive the essential knowledge and skills for entrepreneurial thinking and mindset as well as basic knowledge on marketing, finance, technical, etc. This is followed by a validation phase where start-ups start to explore their customers, market segments, and customer needs in order to provide the proper solution product or service. Start-ups then are being mentored across the prototyping phase and the start-up phase where they are being introduced to the market. The program offers technical and business support, consultation for prototype development, equipped co-working space at the Arab academy, and financial prototyping grant.

    Learn more about the Youth Incubator at EC-AASTMT webpage
