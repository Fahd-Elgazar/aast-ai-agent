biography

Mohamed Sherif is a Lecturer at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). My academic career started after obtaining my Bachelor Degree from the AASTMT – Finance and Accounting Major in 2013 by graduating top of my class with a GPA of 3.99 “Excellent with Honor” and was then appointed as a Teaching Assistant in the Finance and Accounting Department. Afterwards, I obtained my Master of Business Administration (MBA) Degree in Banking and Finance from the AASTMT with a GPA of 4.0. While working as an Assistant Lecturer in the Finance and Accounting Department, my passion for Finance continued to grow and I pursued my research in the areas of Corporate Finance and Financial Markets. I obtained my Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Degree in Business Administration – Finance Major from the AASTMT in September 2024 and got promoted to Lecturer in the Finance and Accounting Department from October 2024.
