Institute for Language Studies (ILS)      for further information

As a research-oriented institution with a commitment to providing high-quality education, the Arab Academy has realised the importance of offering Graduate Programmes in Institute for Language Studies (ILS) as part of the Academy's wide services to its thriving postgraduate community.

The ILS offers stimulating academic and professional post-graduate taught programmes with the award of different degrees, such as certificates, diplomas and masters.

The ILS focuses on the needs of the postgraduate students, providing them with an opportunity to develop their own ideas and interests and to be engaged in critical thinking and career development plans. In addition, it enables students to be part of the research community in their area of specialisation and the wider academic and professional community at large.

The graduate programmes are as follows:
  • Academic Diploma in Applied Linguistics.
  • Diploma in Applied Linguistics .
  • Diploma for teaching Arabic to non-native speakers .
