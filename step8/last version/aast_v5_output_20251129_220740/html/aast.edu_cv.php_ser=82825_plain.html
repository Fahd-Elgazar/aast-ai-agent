biography

Professor Sherine Shafik Ahmed Aly at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport, Architectural Engineering and Environmental Design Department. The field of research is concerned with sustainable urban design and environmental design. I participated in a number of conferences and competitions and supervised masters and Ph. D. degree students. Member of the International Association for People Environment Studies (IAPS) and member in the scientific committee for Sustainable Cities in Italy (SC2)
