biography

Ahmed Maher is an Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). I obtained my PhD in Finance from AASTMT in 2022. The academic career was started after obtaining my Bachelor degree from AASTMT - Accounting and Finance Department in 2008, then I have achieved the degree of M.Sc. - Major Banking and Finance from AASTMT in 2012.
