biography

Ahmed Abdel Sadek Mohamed Teaching Assistant – Business Information Systems Department Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) Mr. Ahmed Abdel Sadek Mohamed serves as a Teaching Assistant in the Business Information Systems Department at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). He obtained his Bachelor of Science degree in Management Information Systems in 2018 with an Excellent with Honors distinction and is currently pursuing an Academic Master of Business Administration (MBA) with a major in E-Business. His academic and research interests focus on business analytics, information systems, and the applications of artificial intelligence in business environments. Mr. Ahmed possesses technical proficiency in database development, business intelligence tools, ERP systems, and project management applications. He has published research examining the impact of artificial intelligence on recruitment and selection processes within telecommunication enterprises.
