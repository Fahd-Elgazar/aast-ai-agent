    Grants and Cooperation Agreements – Entrepreneurship Centre

    Scholarships Offered to Students of the Institute of Technical and Professional Studies – Industrial Service Complex

    Community anti-poverty initiatives

    Distribution of Iftar meals and dates at University Children’s Hospital (April 2024)

    In line with its social responsibility and commitment to SDG 1: No Poverty, the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) continues to promote social inclusion and community engagement through charitable initiatives and outreach programs. In April 2024, AASTMT hosted children from the Mawada and Mercy Orphanage—a group of 15 children and 5 supervisors—at its Abu Qir campus for a Sports Day event organized by the University’s Community Service Department. This activity offered underprivileged children a day of recreation, care, and interaction in a supportive educational environment. While indirect, such initiatives contribute to the broader goals of poverty alleviation by enhancing the emotional well-being, social integration, and self-esteem of disadvantaged children. By extending its resources to serve vulnerable groups, AASTMT fosters a culture of compassion, inclusion, and empowerment, thereby reinforcing its role in supporting the social dimensions of poverty reduction and promoting equitable opportunities for all members of the community.

    ​​​​​​​Distribution of Iftar Meals – Community Service Initiative (February 2024)

    On 26 February 2024, students from Academy Al-Kheir CLC carried out a community service initiative at the University Children’s Hospital in El Shatby, Alexandria. As part of their Ramadan outreach activities, the students distributed Iftar meals to the families of young patients and offered dates at the time of breaking the fast, contributing to a supportive and compassionate environment for those spending long hours in the hospital

    ​​​​​​​Academy AlKheir - CLC, AASTMT

    Distribution of Iftar Meals – Community Service Initiative (April 2024)

    On 4 April 2024, a group of college students participated in the “Iftar for Fasting People” initiative by distributing Iftar meals and clothing to families in El Maamoura El Balad Village. This activity formed part of their Ramadan community outreach efforts, aiming to support families in need and promote solidarity during the holy month.

    Cooperation Between the Entrepreneurship Center and The Medium, Small And Micro Enterprise Development Authority (MSME) 

    The joint memorandum of Understanding – in 2022 and current till now - aims at supporting projects in the entrepreneurship sector during the coming years between the Entrepreneurship Center at the Academy and the authority for the development of medium, small and micro enterprises. The cooperation aims to discover the skills of students, develop their abilities, spread the culture of entrepreneurship, self-employment, qualify graduates and students of the university to establish and manage their projects successfully, as well as adopt innovative and creative ideas among young people, and encourage them to create new job opportunities for them; meet the needs of the current labor market, through the performance of several activities represented in the establishment of training courses, exhibitions, forums; for the development of entrepreneurial skills, workshops for innovation and creativity.

    Encouraging young people to implement small projects based on innovative new ideas capable of competition organizing events that contribute to raising the capabilities of university students in the field of entrepreneurship, empowering young people for self-employment; in implementation of the state's directives and achieving the goals of sustainable development.

    Cooperation with KIT, The Karlsruhe Institute of Technology [2]

    A joint cooperation and sponsorship agreement between the Entrepreneurship Center at the academy and KIT, The Karlsruhe Institute of Technology- TECHgrow program, the support provided to programs organized by the Entrepreneurship Center.

    TECHgrow is a two-phase module, with the first phase taking place in the African partner country. Participants take part in a custom-made accelerator, which leads to a pitch event including a nomination in front of investors. The winners go on a soft landing trip to Germany as part of the second phase. 

    Target group: Start-ups in the roll-out phase in the market

    Cooperation Between the Entrepreneurship Center and The Medium, Small And Micro Enterprise Development Authority (MSME) 

    A joint memorandum of Understanding aimed at supporting projects in the entrepreneurship sector during the coming years between the Entrepreneurship Center at the academy and the authority for the development of medium, small and micro enterprises. The cooperation aims to discover the skills of students, develop their abilities, spread the culture of entrepreneurship, self-employment, qualify graduates and students of the university to establish and manage their projects successfully, as well as adopt innovative and creative ideas among young people, and encourage them to create new job opportunities for them; meet the needs of the current labor market, through the performance of several activities represented in the establishment of training courses, exhibitions, forums; for the development of entrepreneurial skills, workshops for innovation and creativity.

    Campaign to protect the poor and workers from rain and cold (distribution of tiles and socks) (December 1-7, 2021)
