biography

I am a dynamic and dedicated professional with a passion for finance and education. Currently serving as an Assistant Lecturer in the Finance department at AASTMT, I bring a wealth of experience and enthusiasm to the academic realm. Academic Background: I hold a Master's degree in Finance from AASTMT, where I cultivated a solid foundation in financial principles and analytical skills. My academic journey fueled my curiosity for the intricate workings of the financial world, inspiring a commitment to continuous learning and growth. Teaching Philosophy: As an educator, I believe in the transformative power of education to shape minds and empower individuals. My teaching philosophy revolves around creating an inclusive and interactive learning environment where students can not only grasp theoretical concepts but also apply them to real-world scenarios. By fostering critical thinking and encouraging active participation, I aim to instil in my students the skills and knowledge necessary for success in the dynamic field of finance.
