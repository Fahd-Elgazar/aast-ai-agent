The Graduate Programmes in Linguistic and Translation Studies (GPLTS)              for further information

As a research-oriented institution with a commitment to providing high-quality education, the Arab Academy has realised the importance of offering Graduate Programmes in The Graduate Programmes in Linguistic and Translation Studies(GPLTS) as part of the Academy's wide services to its thriving postgraduate community.

The GPLTS offers stimulating academic and professional post-graduate taught programmes with the award of different degrees, such as certificates, diplomas and masters.

The GPLTS focuses on the needs of the postgraduate students, providing them with an opportunity to develop their own ideas and interests and to be engaged in critical thinking and career development plans. In addition, it enables students to be part of the research community in their area of specialisation and the wider academic and professional community at large.

The graduate programmes are as follows:
  • Masters in English for Specific Purposes.
  • Masters of Science in Computer Engineering
