Transfer Regulation

  • Colleges
  • College of Engineering & Technology
  • Transfer Regulation

Transfer applications from accredited academies, universities and colleges are accepted by the College. Equivalent courses are transferred in terms of their credit hours provided that the student’s grade is not less than (Good).

The College of Engineering accepts transferred students from engineering colleges of other institutes or universities according to the following terms:

  • Fulfilling acceptance conditions of the Academy with recognition of the period spent by the student in the faculty of transfer.
  • It is possible to account some of the courses that the student studied as transferred courses provided that it resembles the courses taught in the Academy.
  • Transferred courses grades is not included in the calculation of the accumulative average for the student but is calculated as credit hours within the student’s graduation requirements.
  • The transferred student ought to accomplish in the Academy 50% at least of the required needed hours to obtain the certificate or the degree applied for.
