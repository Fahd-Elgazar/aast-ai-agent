biography

Since childhood, I've harbored a profound curiosity for learning and embracing novelty. This inclination has shaped my perspective on education, viewing it not merely as a routine but as a lifelong pursuit. Encouraged by this habit, I continuously strive to enhance my academic aptitude. This commitment marked the inception of my quest to refine my abilities in teaching and research, aiming to establish a reputable presence as a professor in my field.
