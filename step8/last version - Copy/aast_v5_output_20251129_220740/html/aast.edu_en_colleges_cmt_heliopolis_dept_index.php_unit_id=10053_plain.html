Market & Career
Opportunities

  • Corporate Social Responsibility (CRS) Consultant

    CSR consultant work with companies to develop and implement strategies that align with sustainable development goas and improve their social and environmental impact. 

  • Sustainable Supply Chain Manager 

    Sustainable supply chain managers work to ensure that companies' supply chains are environmentally and socially responsible, minimizing their impact on the environment and society.

  • Sustainability analyst: 

    Sustainability analysts work to assess the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance of companies and provide recommendation for improving sustainability practices.
