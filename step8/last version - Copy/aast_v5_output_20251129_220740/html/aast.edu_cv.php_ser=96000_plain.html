biography

Mohamed Soliman Halawa received his Ph.D. degree in telecommunications engineering from the University of Vigo Spain in 2021 and is currently an Assistant professor at the Department of Business Information Systems, Arab Academy for Science Technology and Maritime Transport, Cairo, Egypt. His recent research interest includes data analysis and recommended systems.
