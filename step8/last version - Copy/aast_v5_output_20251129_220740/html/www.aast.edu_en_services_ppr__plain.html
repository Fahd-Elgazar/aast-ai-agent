Welcome

Protocol, Public Relations & Communications is a distinctive management function that helps establish and maintain mutual lines of communications, understanding, acceptance, and cooperation between AASTMT and the public. The Protocol, Public Relation & Communications Affairs (PPRCA) at AASTMT provides a broad range of services that help AASTMT community outreach and meeting marketing and communications goals. PPRC promotes a positive and consistent image of AASTMT towards internal and external audiences.
