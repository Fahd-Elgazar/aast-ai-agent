Integrated Simulators Complex

who we are ?

The Integrated Simulators Complex (ISC) is committed to maintaining its leadership role in providing bi-lingual simulation training and learning to multinational professionals and students within marine and oil industries in the Middle East Region and the rest of the world

Promoting your Professional Skills
More
History
We are proud of our history and that we have been delivering training to seafarers for over 20 years. Our heritage and values have steered generations of seafarers into successful careers
Vision
We strive to maintain the leadership role in providing simulation training.
Mission
By utilizing top-of-the-line simulation technology, ISC provides up-to-date training and consultation services in various Marine, Environment Protection and Crisis Management, Communications, and Oil Industries fields.
