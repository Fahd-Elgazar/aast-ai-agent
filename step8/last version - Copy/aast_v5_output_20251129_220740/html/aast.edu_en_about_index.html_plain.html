50 Years of Excellence

  • History

    Over more than 50 years, AASTMT had many achievements in education, training and research in the fields of maritime transport, engineering and management sciences. This is due to the methodological application of modern technology in these fields. The AASTMT was thus able to move from regionalism to internationalism.

President Word

Prof. Ismail Abdel Ghafar Ismail Farag

Welcome and thank you for your interest in the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). Since its inception in 1972, AASTMT has been dedicated to fostering a highly professional environment of advancing knowledge, developing human potential and breaking new intellectual and academic ground with the aim of improving the lives of human beings and of communities all over the Arab and the whole World.

Words from

Esteemed Personalities

"Working so closely with a world class institution like the Arab Academy has been one of the highlights of my 17 years in Egypt. Inspiring leadership, high caliber, dedicated staff, creativity, professionalism and openness, and above all, a commitment to education."

Mrs. Caroline Alcock

Her Britannic Majesty's Consul General

"I was glad to visit the simulators building which has no parallel in the whole world. I have also watched the sea race of the students feeling happy and proud of the progress the Academy has achieved in all the fields as well as the evident discipline of the students. This would not have been accomplished without a disciplined, rational and organized leadership."

H.E. Lieutenant General Osama Rabie

Commander in Chief of the Navy

"The Academy is distinct in our Arab world for the high level of its students in all the specializations the Academy offers. We signed a memorandum of understanding between the Academy and the Ministry of Public Works and Transport in Lebanon to supervise and cooperate with the Ministry’s Maritime Institute to train the seamen and run the Institute."

H.E. Mr. Ghazi Ze’aiter

Minister of Public Works and Transport Lebanon
