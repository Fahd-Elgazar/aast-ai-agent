    1.4.3

    The Arab Academy has provided a variety of training courses and programs to improve access to basic services to eradicate poverty in all its forms everywhere in the Academic year 2023/ 2024. Those training courses and programmes aim to provide direct access to services for the community.

    Expansion of collaboration between AASTMT and Fortinet (Global Cybersecurity Company)

    In May 2024, the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) expanded its collaboration with Fortinet, a global leader in cybersecurity solutions, to strengthen students’ access to industry-recognized digital skills. The initiative introduced Fortinet Network Security Expert (NSE) certification courses, established a cybersecurity laboratory on campus, and provided free exam vouchers and training workshops to students within the Faculties of Engineering and Computer Science.

    By offering globally accredited certifications at no additional cost, this partnership directly supports SDG 1 (No Poverty) by removing financial barriers to high-demand professional qualifications and enhancing the employability and income-generating potential of students, particularly those from low-income backgrounds. Through the creation of equitable access to digital education and workforce-ready skills, AASTMT contributes to poverty reduction by fostering inclusive economic opportunities in Egypt’s growing technology sector.

    Community Service and Continuing Education Programmes during 2023/ 2024

    The Community Service and Continuing Education Sector at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) represents a core component of the institution’s social responsibility and its contribution to SDG 1: No Poverty. Through the Center for Adult and Continuing Education, AASTMT offers a wide range of professional, technical, and personal development programs to the public, with discounts exceeding 50% to ensure affordability for learners from low-income and marginalized backgrounds. These non-degree, non-credit courses equip individuals of all ages with practical, career-oriented skills that enhance employability and income-generation capacity across diverse sectors in Egypt and the Arab region. By facilitating lifelong learning and human capital development, AASTMT’s continuing education programs directly support poverty reduction through skills empowerment, social inclusion, and equitable access to education and training opportunities.

    Courses provided by the Community Service and Continuing Education Center during 2022/ 2023


    The Community Service and Continuing Education are an integral part of AASTMT, as it is the social corporate responsibility of AASTMT where it provides charged services to serve the community at large with discounts exceeding 50%. The Center for Adult and Continuing Education is considered to be one of the most highly acclaimed training centers directed to aid the companies and business organizations in Egypt and the Arab World. It has been successful in

    reaching the highest level of proficiency and efficiency. Training is given to prepare trainees in various work fields and different managerial levels, to enable them to develop adequate and move precise managerial and technical policies so that their organizations can become more competitive and adaptable to market changes. Its Programs are established to provide general education and career-oriented courses to the general public, as well as to public and private organizations, companies and entities seeking human resource enrichment for their employees. Its Programs are committed to the principle of Clearing is a lifelong process. The activities should make a direct contribution to the education and well-being of Egypt and the Middle East.

    Therefore, it offers opportunities for people of all ages to participate in non-credit, non-degree, professional training programs and personal enhancement courses in a variety of formats, specifically designed to meet the needs of both local and expatriate learners.
