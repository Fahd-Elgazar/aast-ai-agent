biography

Dr. Hend Abdelmoghny is a distinguished scholar with a rich academic background in finance and business administration. She began her academic journey at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport, where she earned a Bachelor's degree with a specialization in Accounting and Finance. Building on her passion for finance, Dr. Abdelmoghny pursued a Master of Science in Investment at the same institution. Her master's thesis, titled "Obstacles that Affect Long-term Deposits in Egyptian Islamic Banking," highlighted her keen interest in the nuances of Islamic finance. Hend's academic pursuits continued with a PhD from Ain Shams University, Faculty of Business Administration. Her doctoral research focused on "Measuring the Role of Competitive Strategies on the Relationship Between Green Supply Chain and Firm's Performance," reflecting her commitment to exploring the intersection of sustainability and corporate strategy.
