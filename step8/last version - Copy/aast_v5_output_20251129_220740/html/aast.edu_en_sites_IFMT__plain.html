    Welcome to ( IFMT )

    The Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport is organizing an international forum to be attended by all parties involved in the maritime transport industry with the aim of discussing a proposal for establishing the International Forum for Maritime Transport (IFMT), which realizes a partnership between education, training and the maritime transport market at the Arab and International levels.


    Training Courses

    The Center for Maritime Graduates Affairs at the Arab Academy - Abu Qir announces the holding of a set of courses in order to enrich its role in keeping up with the requirements of the labor market for students and graduates of the College of Maritime Transport to improve and develop alot of effective skills for marine officers and engineers , as follows:

    • - Practical Skills for Deck officer
    • - English language courses
    • - Personal and Communication soft skill courses
