biography

I am a Lecturer at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). I started my academic career after obtaining my Bachelor degree from AASTMT - Electronic Commerce Department, in 2009 at the top of my classmates with a GPA of 3.98 – “Excellent with Honor”. I pursued my graduate studies at the same department, and obtained the degree of M.Sc. in Information Systems with a GPA of 3.96. I was then appointed at the Business Information Systems Department of AASTMT in the post of Assistant Lecturer, through which I continued working on my research in areas related to electronic learning learning analytics, and self-regulated learning. I obtained my doctorate degree in Information and communication technologies from the University of Vigo, Spain at April 2023. I am a professional member and journal reviewer of several research and technical organizations, such as IEEE, and Academia.
