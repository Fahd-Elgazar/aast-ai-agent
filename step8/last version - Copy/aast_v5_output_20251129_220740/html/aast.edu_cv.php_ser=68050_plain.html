biography

Dr. Nouran Nabil is an accomplished academic with a rich background in finance and higher education. She began her educational journey at Orouba Language School, later earning her Bachelor's and MBA degrees in Finance from the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT). Pursuing further academic excellence, she received her PhD from the University of Huddersfield in the United Kingdom. Dr. Nouran has been an integral part of AASTMT since 2007, contributing significantly to both academic and administrative roles. In 2012, she was appointed Head of the Cardiff International Program Committee at AASTMT’s Dokki campus, showcasing her leadership in international academic collaboration. She has also served as a dedicated member of the Schedule Entering Committee since 2018. Her commitment to academic quality and student success is further reflected in her current role as Head of the Exams Committee since 2023. Additionally, she became a member of Kortext committee in 2024, supporting digital learning initiatives and innovative education platforms.
