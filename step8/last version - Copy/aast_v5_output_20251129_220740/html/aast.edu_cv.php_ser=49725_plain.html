biography

I am enthusiastic academic with a considerable (More than 20 Years) teaching, research, leadership and management experience in the higher education sector. I have more than Twenty years of national and international teaching and research experience in academic accounting programmes in higher/further educational institutions in Egypt and the UK. Having previously held full-time/part-time lectureship posts in Accounting and Finance at Helwan University, , Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport, as well as University of London International Programs- University of London. (under academic direction of London School of Economics and political Science) I have earned my PhD degree in Accounting (Minor: Auditing) from University of Huddersfield Business School in the UK. Also, I possess a higher education certificate from the University of Huddersfield, UK following a Teaching Assistant Preparation Program (TAPP), an MSc in Accounting from Helwan University, Egypt and BSc with First Class Honours from University of Helwan, Egypt.
