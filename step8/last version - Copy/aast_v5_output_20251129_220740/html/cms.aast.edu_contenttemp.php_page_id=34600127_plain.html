Basic & Applied Sciences Department

 

Basic and Applied Sciences laboratories supply the practical knowledge for a complete understanding of the physics and chemistry subjects and connect practice to theory. Laboratory (1) provides first-year engineering students with hands-on experience of the basic concepts of electricity and trains them on data acquisition and analysis. Laboratory (2) provides first-year engineering students with hands-on experience of the basic concepts of heat and waves. The chemistry laboratory provides first-year engineering students with practical training in chemistry with engineering applications.
