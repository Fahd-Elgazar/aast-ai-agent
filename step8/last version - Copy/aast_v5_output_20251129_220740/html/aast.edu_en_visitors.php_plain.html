Welcome to AASTMT
Going to college is a big step and we at Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) recognize the investment this requires as a student and as a parent or family member. Students often face challenges which may overwhelm or confuse them, at AASTMT we can make such obstacles easier to overcome. AASTMT is committed to working with its students to help them learn and achieve their goals independently.
