biography

Prof. Alfaisal A. Hasan holds the post of Professor of Engineering Mathematics in the Basic and Applied Sciences Department - College of Engineering and Technology Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport – South Vally Branch (AASTMT). He is Dean of Admission and Registration. Additionally, Dr. Alfaisal A. Hasan was the Head of the Department since November 2013. He was Vice Director for Administrative Affairs. He obtained his Doctorate in Engineering Mathematics from the Faculty of Engineering, Ain-Shams University. He has more than 46 international Journal papers in the field of Applied Mathematics. He has attended and presented papers in more than 27 international conferences. Dr. Alfaisal A. Hasan is a member of the following societies IAENG, WASET, EMS and IEEMS. He had Awarded Professor Dr Attia Ashor in Mathematics 2017 and Professor Dr Amin Lotfy in Mathematics 2018 - Academy of Scientific Research, Egypt. He had Awarded The Minister of Interior Excellence – First Place Best Consultant and Expert, United Arab Emirates 2019.
