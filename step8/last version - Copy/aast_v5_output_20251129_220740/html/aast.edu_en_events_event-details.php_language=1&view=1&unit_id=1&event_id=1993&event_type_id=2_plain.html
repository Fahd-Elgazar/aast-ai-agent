Arab Dialogue Circle on Artificial Intelligence in the Arab World

  • by: Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport
  • AASTMT Campus
  • Conference

The Arab Dialogue Circle on "Artificial Intelligence in the Arab World" is set to take place on February 2-3, 2025, at the Headquarters of the General Secretariat of the Arab League States in Cairo. This pivotal event is jointly organized by the Arab Academy for Science Technology and Maritime Transport alongside the Naif Arab University for Security Sciences. The dialogue aims to foster collaborative discussions on the opportunities and challenges posed by artificial intelligence in the region, bringing together policymakers, industry leaders, and academics. Participants will delve into innovative AI applications, ethical considerations, and strategies for enhancing digital literacy across Arab nations. By creating a platform for knowledge exchange, this event seeks to empower the Arab world to leverage AI for sustainable development and economic growth. The outcomes of the dialogue will undoubtedly contribute to shaping a shared vision for the future of technology in the Arab region.
