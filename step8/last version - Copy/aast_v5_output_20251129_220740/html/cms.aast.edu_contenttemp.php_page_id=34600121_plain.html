Computer Engineering Department

The laboratories of the computer engineering department offer students state of the art equipment to further their study in the department and prepare them for work in the market. Our computer laboratories contain top of the line machines that allow students to use the most compute intensive software packages required by their major and our specialized laboratories contain state of the art equipment to further education in robotics, digital design, embedded systems and microprocessor design.
