Marketing and international Business -Heliopolis

Objectives

Objectives

·         Realize the distinguished level and precedence in education.

·         Recruit and retain academically prepared and diverse student body

·         Recruit the nationally and globally recognized faculty members.

·         Develop and support prominent undergraduate and graduate programs that satisfy the national and international requirements.

·         Realize the distinguished level and precedence in research.

·         Encourage student volunteer activities and community service.

·         Develop infrastructure and financial resources.

Values

·         Excellence and devotion in education.

·         Democracy, mutual respect and integrity.

·         Academic research transparency.

·         Equality and justice.

·         Social responsibility.

 

Program Map

1. Program Title

·         Marketing & International Business

2. Intended Learning Outcomes

2-1. Knowledge and Understanding

o    Know the scientific methods to resolve problems and take decisions on different managerial levels.

o    Understand the foundations for preparing plans, programs, and policies in accordance with the institution’s requirements.

o    Perceive the foundations of marketing feasibility studies and evaluate investment proposals.

o    Learn the foundations of designing organizational structures, systems and administrative manuals for the various activities in the institution.

o    Understand the modern managerial philosophies such as total quality management and restructuring and taking advantage of opportunities and applying it in different work settings.

o    Learn principles of leadership and influencing others.

o    Understand organizational communication and work relationships.

o    Understand principles of motivation of others.

o    Know the principles and concepts of policies, strategies and plans and requirements used to meet the expectations of organizational stakeholders.

2-2. Intellectual Skills

o    Ability to practice creative and innovative thinking when facing different managerial situations.

o    Link between environmental changes and decision making patterns.

o    Preparing scenarios, strategies, and different models to deal with managerial issues and situations.

2-3. Professtional/Practical Skills

o    Prepare strategic plans and working plans and policies

o    Prepare and design different work systems.

o    Design and develop organizational hierarchies.

o    Design and manage management information systems and databases.

o    Prepare feasibility studies and project evaluation.

o    Prepare marketing and sales plans. Financial analysis and investment projects evaluation.

o    Analysis of Markets.

o    Prepare plans and programs for usage, development and maintenance of financial and human resources, as well as other resources.

o    The practical application of modern scientific methods and systems in the field of Business Administration.

2-4. Transferable/Key Skills

o    Learn effective time management.

o    Practice effective communication and influencing others.

o    Practice team work.

o    Apply cognitive and intellectual development and continuous self-learning.

o    Learn methods and techniques for solving problems with high competency on individual or institutional level .

o    Gain presentational skills and dialogue.

o    Learn self-management and dealing with work pressure.

o    Practice innovation, development and continues improvement in work.

o    Use terminologies related to program topics.

o    Proficient use of a widely used foreign language
