H.E. Judge Boulos Fahmy

President of the Egyptian Supreme Constitutional Court
Published on MAY 15, 2025

May 15, 2025, marks a historic and unforgettable day for the Supreme Constitutional Court, its members, and above all, its President, who had the honor of visiting an esteemed Arab-Egyptian institution led by His Excellency Prof. Dr. Ismail Abdel Ghaffar. It is truly a source of pride to witness such a distinguished scientific establishment on Egyptian soil—one that masterfully blends tradition with modernity. I must admit, when I first read about the Academy and later met Prof. Ismail, who shared its remarkable vision, I never imagined that Egypt housed such a comprehensive and advanced academic system, rivaling the world’s most prestigious institutions.
