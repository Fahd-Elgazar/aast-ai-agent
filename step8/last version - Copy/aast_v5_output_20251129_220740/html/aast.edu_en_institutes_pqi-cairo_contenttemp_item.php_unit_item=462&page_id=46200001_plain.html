Training Depratment


The intention of the PQI training services is to provide integrated courses that are primarily based on learning experiences.
Courses include relevant aspects thus attaining greater commitment and higher learning impact for the participants who are encouraged to contribute during discussions.
All training courses are conducted either at the PQI premises in Cairo, or in-house at the client''s premises. According to the broad range of the clients'' needs, we tailor, develop and deliver specified training courses as per your request supported by highly competent tutors.
Training courses are developed and designed to provide the full range of interests and objectives of participants'' at all organizational levels and to meet the different skill gaps
- Special tailored courses
- Internationally registered courses
- Practical and Hands-on experience
- Flexibility on venue, timings, and dates
- High quality training material and equipment
