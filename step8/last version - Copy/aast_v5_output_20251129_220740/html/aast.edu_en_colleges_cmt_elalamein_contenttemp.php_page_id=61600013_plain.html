Open Talk with Businessman and Entrepreneur/ Ahmed Abu Hashima (March 7, 2023)

The branch of the Arab Academy in the new city of El Alamein hosted His Excellency Representative / Ahmed Abu Hashima, businessman and industrialist, Chairman of the Youth and Sports Committee, and the founder of Abu Hashima Al-Khair, the sponsor of the Abu Hashima Start-up Competition. Sustainability Session (February 26, 2023)

The Academy's branch in El Alamein embraces the Arab Sustainability Day and that is under the support of His Excellency Prof. Dr. Ismail Abdel Ghaffar Ismail Farag, President of the Arab Academy, Hosted by the College of Management and Technology, El Alamein Branch. It's a remarkable opportunity for startups and technology enthusiasts to meet investors, mentors and public officials from all over the world.

Your Career Starts Here Session (October 26, 2022)

College of Management and Technology Alamein was pleased to host a session on Tuesday the 25th of October 2022 about “Your Career Starts Here” by Ms. Nouran Elzuheiry, Talent Acquisition Officer at Ebank. The session discussed topics about how to write a CV, how to deal with labor market and how to deal during personal interviews aiming to support students and develop their skills for labor market.

     

"Meet the Best Version of Yourself" Session (October 17, 2022)

College of Management and Technology Alamein hosted today, Monday the 17th of October 2022, a session about “Meet the Best Version of Yourself” by Dr. Iman Fekri - Career Coach and Mr. Abdallah Rabiaa - ICF Certified Coach and Specialist in Self-Development. The session discussed topics about the future vision, self-discovery, soft skills and their importance in social and professional life. Also, it addressed ways to improve self-confidence, communication skills and balancing personal and professional life. Guiding our students to access knowledge and insights to help reinforcing their professional identity as today’s conversations create tomorrow’s opportunities.

     

Sports Management Session (October 12, 2022)

College of Management and Technology Alamein hosted a session by Mr. Marwan Rashed, PR Manager at Alex Runners, talking about sports management the field of business that focuses directly on sports and recreation. Alex Runners, Alexandria Marathon for Organizing Events, has organized a lot of sporting and charitable events in the recent period, like the Half Marathon and the Alexandria Marathon for a distance of 21 km in 2017/2018 and 2020/2021 and Alexandria Sports Festival in cooperation with Alexandria Governorate, including an awareness run for the “C” virus in partnership with the “C” treatment center at the Faculty of Medicine of Alexandria University. They seek to change the sports culture of the Alexandrian people and push them to exercise freely on the Alexandria Corniche, so that they enjoy better health and style and encouraging them to get rid of unhealthy habits that necessarily affect all aspects of life.

  

Career Development Center Session "Prepare for the Future" (October 11,2022)

Banlastic Session (May 30, 2022)

College of Management and Technology Alamein hosted on Monday the 30th of May 2022, a session about “Plastic Pollution and Climate Change.” The session was represented by Mr. Ahmed Yassin co-founder and marketing strategist of Banlastic Egypt. Banlastic Egypt is social enterprise based in Alexandria that tackles the plastic pollution problem and aims to ban single use of plastic.

Through the session Mr. Ahmed Yassin discussed the harms of plastic; from not being able to biodegrade, to breaking down into small pieces and entering our food chain, harming marine life by being ingested and tangled in and finally how it’s expected that by 2050, there will be more plastic in the sea than Fish. Sequentially, Banlastic is aiming to ban the single use plastics in Egypt to contribute in the global action towards the plastic catastrophe and the sustainable development goals 2030.

     

Entrepreneurship Session (May 9, 2022)

It was held today at College of Management and Technology Alamein a session about “Entrepreneurship” presented by Dr. Hossam Khaled El-Abd - Chairman & CEO at Elabd Chemical Industries Company. During the session Dr. Hossam El-Abd explained what is entrepreneurship, who is an entrepreneur and why entrepreneurship. He also shared with students examples for entrepreneurs through the years and how they succeeded to achieve their accomplishments. The session further discussed some of the theories about Entrepreneurship, it’s importance and what are the characteristics of an entrepreneur. Besides, the different types of entrepreneurships and what may causes its failure.

Gamification Session (February 28, 2022)

The session discussed “Gamification” and how it can be defined as the application of game-design elements and game principles in non-games contexts to solve problems by using or applying the characteristics of game elements.
