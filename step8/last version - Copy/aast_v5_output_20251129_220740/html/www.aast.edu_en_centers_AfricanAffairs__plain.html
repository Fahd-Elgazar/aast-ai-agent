African and Asian Affairs in Brief

Africa is considered a major element in the strategic plan of AASTMT since its establishment in 1972.In specific, AASTMT targets to excel the education, research and capacity building in the African continent. The African and Asian Affairs Sector was established as an executive sector targeting the vision of having outstanding educational and research in Africa.
