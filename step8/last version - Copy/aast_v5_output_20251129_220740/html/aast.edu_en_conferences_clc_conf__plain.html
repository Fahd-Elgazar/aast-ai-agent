Introduction

College of Language and Communication (CLC), Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT - Egypt), holds its 2nd International Conference (LANACOM) titled “Interdisciplinary Studies in the Digital Era: Innovation and Digitization in Language, Culture and Communication”

LANACOM is an invitation to researchers, academicians, practitioners and educators from different backgrounds and cultures to exchange and share their experience and research skills on digital media, natural language processing, digital literature, culture, cinema and filmmaking, and discuss the most recent trends, innovations and practical challenges in the digital era.

Visiting Alexandria

Visiting Alexandria, once the greatest cultural center of the world, will definitely be an unforgettable experience.
This Mediterranean gem has always played an important role in preserving and transmitting cultures.
For this reason, Alexandria has been considered a cosmopolitan city, belonging more to the Mediterranean world than to its hinterland.
It is Egypt’s second-largest city and boasts a thriving port along its 32 kilometers of coastline.


Alexandria embraces various touristic sites and museums, ranging from the ancient Roman theater, the medieval citadel of Qaitbay, the Royal Montazah Palace, to Bibliotheca Alexandrina.
In addition, it is a site for a one-of-a-kind underwater archeological museum where thousands of ancient ruins have been discovered.
