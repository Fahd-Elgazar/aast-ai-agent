    AASTMT Sports Services

    who we are ?

    As a firm believer in the holistic development of our students, we recognize the transformative power of sports in fostering teamwork, discipline, leadership, and resilience. Our sports services are curated to cater to the diverse interests and skill levels of our student body, providing a platform for both recreational enthusiasts and aspiring athletes to flourish.

    Sports Competitions
    The Academy is interested in recreational competitions that contribute to the development of students' abilities within groups and individual games.
    Organizing Sports Championships
    Organizing many international and local sports tournaments
    Community Service
    The Academy plays a major role in supporting the community in serving the environment, such as the youth camp in Abu Qir and the schools surrounding the Academy
