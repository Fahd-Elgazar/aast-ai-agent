3. Data Scope and Scraping SolutionsData ScopeThe project explicitly targets every type of information available on the AAST website.  This includes campuses, colleges, departments, programs, courses, tuition fees, grading systems, admission rules, academic bylaws, staff pages, administrative pages, and PDF attachments.  Any published information is considered part of the knowledge domain.  Scraping IssuesInconsistent Architecture: The AAST website consists of 7+ independent subsystems (e.g., legacy CMS, Staff CV system, React/Vue training centers), resulting in no standard URL structure and causing traditional crawlers to fail.  Dynamic Navigation: Menus are JavaScript-driven, meaning links only appear after a hover or click action.  API Instability: Key APIs exhibit unstable behaviors such as changing JSON structures, random redirects, and null responses.  HTML Noise: Pages contain over 20 JS files, repeated headers, analytics scripts, and hidden DOM blocks requiring heavy cleaning.  Five-Phase Scraping SolutionManual Seed Discovery: Defining college, admission, training, and service pages manually.  Headful Playwright Crawling: Simulating interactions such as hovering menus, clicking dropdowns, and waiting for AJAX completion.  Multi-Endpoint Extraction: Trying APIs first, falling back to rendered HTML, manually triggering AJAX, and brute-forcing ID-based discovery.  Cleaning & Deduplication: Executing URL canonicalization, content hashing, language normalization, and removing headers/footers.  Normalization: Mapping all content into structured entities (campuses, colleges, programs, etc.).
