# ICCTA 2026 rehearsal shortlist

Mohammed and Fahd approve the sequence together after the conference branches are reviewed and the exact candidate passes the isolated smoke. The purpose is to show evidence-scoped academic advising with a short follow-up. Scripted Decision data is used only to prove service startup.

| # | Prompt | What to check during rehearsal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which bachelor’s programs does the College of Artificial Intelligence offer at El Alamein? | Establish the two verified program names in a new guest conversation. |
| 2 | What careers can the first one lead to? | Resolve the first program and establish the career topic intentionally. |
| 3 | Does the other one include Machine Learning? | Stay on the same conversation. Resolve Data Science and preserve the Machine Learning membership question. Use evidence or explain the gap for that program. |
| 4 | Does the graph recommend Computer Vision after Machine Learning? | Demonstrate a course sequence check with both named courses, distinct from a prerequisite or major recommendation. |
| 5 | What are the merit scholarship eligibility criteria? | Refer to the applicable scholarship evidence. Keep eligibility separate from application deadlines and procedures. |
| 6 | Who issues the official academic calendar? | Identify the authority from the calendar source and show the citation. |
| 7 | Does Mobile Computing have a recorded syllabus? | Establish the course and evidence before the follow-up. |
| 8 | What about week 3 of that course? | Preserve both week 3 and Mobile Computing in the same conversation. Show a specific explanation if week-level evidence is absent. |

Once both developers agree:

1. Merge the reviewed changes in the agreed order and record the exact candidate commit, fixture hashes, image identifiers, model digests and smoke output.
2. Run the approved prompts in the same order on the rehearsal machine. Save the expected evidence or explicit limitation per prompt. Tag that tested commit as `iccta-2026-demo`.
3. Record the rehearsal on the tagged build in a fresh guest session. Keep the browser on the demo, hide credentials and use a local copy of the video for the talk. Play the saved video through once to check it works without the stack.

Mohammed owns the conversation regressions and the integration candidate. Fahd owns the independent cleanup review, evidence retrieval changes and rehearsal verification. See the [fixture profile](profile.json) and [setup guide](../../appliance/README.md).
