# Published audit package

This copy preserves the original diagnostic findings, cleanup manifest, roadmap and case identifiers. The replay has all 125 actually submitted synthetic prompts and expected behaviors in order. The turn and failure indices omit raw model output, retrieved passages, private locators and session/request identifiers. The original audit remains available locally. The publication does not grant access to the underlying academic corpus or operational stores.

Source behavior and timing were measured on the July build named in the reports. Native verification of the synchronized August-based source is recorded separately. Use fresh guest sessions and authorized fixture stores when rerunning the synthetic prompts.
